# Cinematography & Lighting Lexicon

## Lens Aesthetics
- **Anamorphic 35mm**: Horizontal lens flare, oval bokeh, wide 2.39:1 field of view.
- **50mm Prime (Standard)**: Natural human perspective, realistic depth.
- **85mm Portrait**: Compressed background, beautiful isolation of facial expressions.
- **24mm Wide Angle**: Dynamic perspective, spatial expansion, environmental prominence.

## Volumetric & Lighting Styles
- **Chiaroscuro**: High contrast, dramatic shadows (ideal for Noir/Thriller).
- **Practical Neon**: Ambient colored glow from diegetic set lights.
- **Atmospheric Haze / Fog**: Softens highlights, scatters light rays into visible beams.
- **Golden Hour / Magic Hour**: Warm directional 2800K sunlight.
