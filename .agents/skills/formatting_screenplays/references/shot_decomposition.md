# Shot Decomposition & Beat Rules

## Granularity Guidelines
1. **Pacing**:
   - Fast Action: 1.5s - 3.0s per shot.
   - Dialogue / Tension: 3.5s - 6.0s per shot.
   - Establishing / Ambient: 4.0s - 8.0s per shot.

2. **Shot Categorization**:
   - `WS`: Wide Shot / Master
   - `MS`: Medium Shot / Waist-up
   - `MCU`: Medium Close-Up
   - `CU`: Close-Up / Emotional focus
   - `ECU`: Extreme Close-Up / Detail shot
   - `OTS`: Over-The-Shoulder dialogue coverage

3. **Lighting & Palette Prompting**:
   - Specify key light, fill light, and ambient color temperature (e.g. `3200K tungsten key with 5600K cyan neon fill`).
